    <h1 class="legal-page__title">Warranty</h1>
    <p class="legal-page__lead">

    </p>

    <div class="legal-page__card">
      <h2>What’s covered</h2>
      <p>
        Warranty covers hardware faults that occur under normal use during the warranty period.
        If there’s a confirmed fault, we’ll repair, replace, or refund depending on the situation and availability.
      </p>
    </div>

    <div class="legal-page__card">
      <h2>What’s not covered</h2>
      <ul>
        <li>Accidental damage (drops, cracks, water damage)</li>
        <li>Damage caused by misuse, tampering, or unauthorised repairs</li>
        <li>Normal wear and tear (cosmetic marks, battery aging)</li>
        <li>Issues caused by software, third-party apps, or unsupported modifications</li>
      </ul>
    </div>

    <div class="legal-page__card">
      <h2>Battery notes</h2>
      <p>
        Batteries are consumable components. Battery performance naturally reduces over time.
        If your order includes a battery health guarantee, it will be stated on the product page or at checkout.
      </p>
    </div>

    <div class="legal-page__card">
      <h2>How to claim</h2>

        <li>We’ll guide you through quick checks and next steps.</li>
        <li>If needed, we’ll arrange inspection/return instructions.</li>
      </ol>
      <p class="legal-page__muted">
        Keep your proof of purchase - it’s required for warranty claims.
      </p>
